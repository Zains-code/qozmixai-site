The problem

Customers judge your business by its social media. Keeping it active is the hard part.

Inactive channels lose customers

People check your Instagram or blog before they book or buy. A feed that hasn't been updated in months makes them hesitate.

Agencies are expensive. DIY takes hours.

Small businesses either pay an agency every month or spend hours on each post themselves: planning, writing, designing and resizing it for every platform.

Korean platforms are hard for foreign founders

Foreign startups in Korea need content that sounds natural to Korean customers. Machine-translated posts don't.

How it works

From your brand to ready-to-post content in three steps

  1. 1

    Set up your brand once

    Tell us about your business, customers, tone of voice and colours, and upload a few posts you like. QozmixAI learns your brand style.

  2. 2

    Approve your monthly plan

    Get a month of post ideas balanced across educational, promotional and community content. Keep what fits and replace what doesn't.

  3. 3

    Get ready-to-post content

    AI writes the copy, caption and hashtags and lays them out in your brand template as a single post or a carousel. Edit anything, give final approval and export.
