Privacy policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This policy explains how the QozmixAI website (qozmixai.tech) handles your information. QozmixAI is an early-stage startup team based in Seoul, South Korea. Our product is in a pilot phase. It will have its own privacy policy when it launches publicly.

What we collect

  • When you email us: your email address, your name if you include it, and whatever you write to us (for example, details about your business).
  • Language preference: if you switch languages, your choice is saved in your own browser (local storage). It is not sent to us.

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and it has no sign-up forms.

How we use it

We use the information in your email only to reply to you and to discuss the QozmixAI pilot or early access. We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Services we rely on

  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses for security purposes.
  • Google Fonts delivers the fonts on this page, so your browser sends a request to Google when the page loads.
  • Zoho Mail hosts our email inbox.

How long we keep it

We keep emails only as long as we need them to respond to you or work with you during the pilot. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email hello@qozmixai.tech and we will respond as soon as we can.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
